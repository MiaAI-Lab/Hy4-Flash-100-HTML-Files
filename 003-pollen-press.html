POLLENPRESS

Anthemis nocturna, the candle-flower, opens only after the third bell and closes before the baker's cart. Its pollen is said to stain the fingers vermilion for exactly one day. This specimen was pressed between pages 118 and 119 of a ledger that has since gone missing.
