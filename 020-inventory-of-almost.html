The Inventory of Almost

Objects withdrawn before manufacture
1908–1964 · Six surviving drawings

Thirteen marks on the dial. The foreman said nobody could be asked to wait that long.

A cup without a handle, patented in 1922. Withdrawn after the designer tried to hold it.

One tooth, one purpose. It opened a door that was bricked up before the second prototype.

A bell with no clapper. Intended for rooms where silence was part of the service.

A left shoe for the right foot. The factory lost the mirror and never recovered.

A bending ruler for measuring corners. It could not decide which measurement to keep.
