TITLE
Gallery of Failed Machines — A Museum of Almost

PROMPT
A small museum gallery hung in claret #6E2630, with brass #C6A15B fixtures and paper #F1E7D2 plaques. Along the left wall, a vertical index lists five inventions: THE SELF-WINDING STAIR, THE PERPETUAL KETTLE, THE AMBITIOUS PENDULUM, THE HONEST MIRROR, THE LADDER TO YESTERDAY. Each has a year and a one-line reason for failure. Selecting one brings a large line-drawing exhibit to the centre: wheels, belts, pulleys, buckets and little arrows, all drawn in brass on paper. Every machine attempts to work: the stair climbs itself for two steps then tips, the kettle pours into its own fire, the pendulum swings wider until it stops, the mirror reflects a sentence that is not there, the ladder folds into a compact unsuccessful object. After two seconds a red JAM stamp appears over the drawing. Copy under the title: “Five machines that worked perfectly right up to the part that mattered.” Entrance: lights come up over the gallery one at a time and the first machine starts. Signature moment: each failure is mechanically specific, not a generic stop — you can see exactly where the idea gave up. No emoji, no glass, no purple. Reduced motion shows the final jammed state immediately.

DESCRIPTION
A museum of inventions that failed for interesting reasons. Each exhibit runs once, demonstrates its own precise flaw, and stamps itself JAM.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Swappable SVG exhibit drawings, per-machine CSS keyframes with overshoot and collapse, stamp reveal, wall-light entrance, live plaque with real copy.

INTERACTION MODEL
Click or Tab through the index to select an exhibit. Each selection restarts the machine's attempt. Keyboard arrows move through the index.
