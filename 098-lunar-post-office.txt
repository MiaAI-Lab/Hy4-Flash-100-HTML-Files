TITLE
Lunar Post Office — Letters for the Far Side

PROMPT
A black #0D0E10, cream #ECE4D1 and gold #C7A35C night desk. A cream envelope rests under a small moon. Three gold stamps orbit the envelope, then land on its corner and fuse into a wax seal. On load the orbit and landing happen during the first three seconds. Dragging the envelope moves it; the stamps break orbit, chase it and seal again. A printed line reads “TO THE FAR SIDE, VIA THE THIRD QUARTER.” No emoji, no photograph, no purple.

DESCRIPTION
An envelope whose orbiting stamps settle into a wax seal and follow it when dragged.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG envelope and moon, orbital motion, drag tracking, wax seal drawing, Web Audio tick.

INTERACTION MODEL
Drag the envelope to move it. Keyboard: arrows move it, Space releases the stamps again. Reduced motion shows the sealed envelope.
