TITLE
Tachymeter 069 — An Instrument for the Speed of Attention

PROMPT
A black #16171A instrument page with one enormous steel #C7CBCD dial and a single orange #E17628 seconds hand. A tachymeter scale runs around the rim: 60, 120, 180, 240, 300. On load the needle performs a slow self-test sweep and settles at zero. Afterwards it tracks how fast the pointer moves across the page, easing toward each burst and leaving a fading orange trace behind its tip. The knurled bezel can be dragged clockwise or anticlockwise; it rotates the scale and shifts the reading by a small correction. Signature moment: a quick flick of the mouse makes the needle overshoot and the trace briefly loop like a skid mark. No emoji, no watch product photo, no glassmorphism.

DESCRIPTION
A watch dial that measures pointer speed, with a draggable bezel and a persistent orange trace from the needle tip.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG dial and generated tick scale, requestAnimationFrame needle physics, pointer velocity sampling, rotating bezel interaction, fading trace polyline.

INTERACTION MODEL
Move the pointer to drive the needle. Drag the outer bezel to rotate and correct the scale. Keyboard: arrow keys rotate the bezel. Reduced motion moves the needle directly without the trace.
