TITLE
Lantern Street — Nine Stalls After the Rain

PROMPT
A narrow night market seen down a wet street: deep red #4B101A walls, black #130C0D shadows, gold #E7B44B lanterns hung at nine depths. Each lantern is a small paper cylinder with ribs and a dark cap, suspended by a string. On load they sway in a slow staggered sequence, then almost stop. Hovering one lantern makes it swing harder and grows a pool of warm light on the street below, revealing a tiny vendor detail: a tea canister, a mending basket, a stack of sweet-rice bowls, an umbrella stand. Signature moment: a stall appearing out of the dark only where the lantern's light reaches. No emoji, no generic bokeh, no purple.

DESCRIPTION
A night-market street where swinging lanterns cast pools of light that reveal small vendor details.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
CSS paper lanterns with ribbing and perspective scale, staggered sway animation, radial light pools, hidden detail reveals, wet-street reflections.

INTERACTION MODEL
Hover or focus a lantern to swing it and light its stall. Keyboard: lanterns are buttons. Reduced motion keeps lanterns still but hover reveals the light.
