TITLE
The Inventory of Almost — A Cabinet of Objects That Nearly Existed

PROMPT
A flat, frontal filing cabinet occupying the whole page, drawn without perspective. Palette: terracotta #B45F3C cabinet, sage #7C8F7B recesses, cream #F3EADC drawer fronts, ink #2B2A26 type. Six shallow drawers are stacked vertically, each labelled with a handwritten-style serif name and a small inventory number. The title “THE INVENTORY OF ALMOST” sits in the top margin with a subtitle: “Objects withdrawn before manufacture, 1908–1964.” Each drawer face is a button. Opening one slides the cream panel to the right by 58% with a dry mechanical trajectory, revealing a sage recess containing a simple drawn object (a clock with thirteen marks, a cup with no handle, a key with one tooth, etc.) and one sentence explaining why it was not made. Only one drawer is open at a time. A thin ruler printed beside the cabinet shows the opening distance in millimetres and updates as the drawer moves. Entrance: drawers settle into place from top to bottom with tiny differences in overshoot; the ruler draws itself last. Signature moment: when a drawer passes 40 mm, a faint dust line appears along its lower edge and the object is revealed in two drawn strokes. No emoji, no cards, no gradients on text. Reduced motion opens instantly.

DESCRIPTION
A cabinet of rejected inventions. Each drawer contains one object that almost existed and the precise, unromantic reason it was withdrawn.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
CSS transform sliding drawer fronts with custom spring easing, clip-path cavity reveals, CSS-drawn objects, measured ruler beside the cabinet, staged stroke animation, keyboard-accessible buttons.

INTERACTION MODEL
Click a drawer to open it; click again or choose another to close. Arrow keys move between drawers; Escape closes all. The ruler updates with the drawer state.
