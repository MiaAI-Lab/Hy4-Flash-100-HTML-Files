TITLE
X-Ray Type — An Alphabet With Bones

PROMPT
A clinical black #0C1216 specimen drawer containing twenty-six letters. At rest each glyph is only a cold blue-white #D8EDF2 outline. Hovering or focusing a letter illuminates it from inside: vertebrae, ribs and fine hatching appear clipped precisely to the letterform, like a plate held up to light. Clicking pins the letter into a large viewing window on the right, where a scan line travels down the form and labels name the structures: SPINE, RIBS, MARROW, PRESSURE. Palette stays black, blue-white and gray #6F8994. Signature moment: the first illuminated “A”, where the letter's own counter becomes a rib cage. No emoji, no glow blobs, no UI chrome. Reduced motion shows the bones immediately.

DESCRIPTION
A typographic specimen whose letters reveal imagined skeletal interiors when illuminated.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG text as clipPath, clipped rib/spine drawings, staggered grid entrance, custom scan-line animation, precise clinical labels.

INTERACTION MODEL
Hover or focus to preview a letter; click to pin it in the viewing window. Keyboard: letter buttons are tabbable; Enter pins.
