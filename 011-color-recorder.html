Workshop no. 11 · Threaded language

Color Recorder

A loom with no memory for spelling. It remembers only colour: twenty-six letters, six dyes, one shuttle moving left to right.

A–F · kiln red
G–M · saffron dust
N–S · bottle green
T–Z · iron black

Type to weave · Backspace to unweave · Enter to start a fresh warp
