TITLE
Insect Hotel Nocturnal — A Cutaway With Tenants

PROMPT
An isometric cutaway of a wooden insect hotel, drawn as clean SVG polygons on a warm kraft #CBA36C field. Structure in ochre #A86B32 and dark umber #3B3225; tenants in moss #647A4E; one signal colour, lantern yellow #F2D06B. The title “INSECT HOTEL NOCTURNAL” sits upper left in narrow spaced capitals, with a note: “Occupancy recorded at dusk. Tenants are not disturbed by visitors.” The hotel has twelve compartments of different shapes — straw tubes, pinecone scales, drilled blocks, hollow stems — each containing one small resident that moves continuously: a beetle patrols, a spider bobs, a earwig turns in place, a moth opens and closes its wings. Hovering or focusing a compartment lights it and reveals a specimen label in a fixed margin with a precise invented species name and a one-line behaviour. Entrance: compartments slide into place from the bottom of the drawing in three groups. Signature moment: a LIGHTS button turns the whole hotel to night — the wood darkens, every tenant's eyes become two lantern-yellow points, and the drawing briefly becomes a glowing grid. No emoji, no rounded cards, no gradients on type. Reduced motion stops tenant movement but keeps hover labels.

DESCRIPTION
An illustrated habitat that comes alive at dusk. The hotel is both a diagram and a small theatre: each compartment has a tenant with a routine, and night turns the whole structure into a lantern.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Inline SVG isometric polygons, CSS keyframe motion inside SVG groups, focusable compartment groups, live specimen label, night-mode class with simultaneous eye highlights.

INTERACTION MODEL
Hover, focus or click a compartment to read its label; press the Lights button to switch day/night. Keyboard access through named buttons over each compartment.
