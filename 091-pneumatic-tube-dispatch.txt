TITLE
Pneumatic Dispatch — A Tube That Carries Paper

PROMPT
A copper #B86B4B, cream #F0E6D2 and ink #2D2A26 pneumatic dispatch diagram. Four brass stations are linked by a single looping glass tube drawn as a thin double line. On load a capsule whistles from the first station to the second in the opening three seconds, leaving a faint trail in the tube. Clicking another station sends the capsule there along the arc; a small dispatch log records the route and a paper slip appears at the receiving station. No emoji, no office photograph, no purple.

DESCRIPTION
A pneumatic tube network that sends a capsule between brass stations on click.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG path animation with getPointAtLength, double-line tube, capsule drag, dispatch log, Web Audio whistle.

INTERACTION MODEL
Click a station to send the capsule there. Keyboard: 1–4 select stations, Space resends. Reduced motion moves the capsule instantly.
