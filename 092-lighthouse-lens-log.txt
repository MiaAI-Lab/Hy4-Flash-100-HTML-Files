TITLE
The Lens Log — A Lighthouse That Reads Ships

PROMPT
A night-sea #102A3A, amber #F0B451 and chalk #EDE6D4 lighthouse study. A cutaway tower holds a faceted rotating lens; a pale amber beam sweeps over a circular horizon where invisible ships wait at fixed bearings. As the beam crosses each bearing, a ship's name is written into the lens log and glows for a moment on the water. On load the beam swings through the first ship in three seconds. Dragging the lens turns the beam; clicking it advances by a facet. No emoji, no lighthouse photograph, no purple.

DESCRIPTION
A rotating lighthouse beam that reveals ship names at their bearings.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG rotating beam wedge, faceted lens, bearing detection, log entries, Web Audio horn on discovery.

INTERACTION MODEL
Drag the lens to aim the beam or click to step one facet. Keyboard: arrow keys rotate, L log toggles. Reduced motion reveals all ships.
