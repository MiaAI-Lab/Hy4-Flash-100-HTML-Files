TITLE
Deep-Sea Sonar — Contacts at the Edge of the Sweep

PROMPT
A working sonar console in black #07100E with phosphor green #6ED49B and pale #D5E7DD lettering. A circular scope fills most of the screen, crossed by fine range rings and a rotating green sweep whose trailing edge leaves a faint afterglow. Seven contacts drift slowly across the scope: two are marked FISH, one WRECK, one UNKNOWN, and three are unlabelled. Each contact flares briefly when the sweep passes it, then fades to a dim point. A rotary dial below the scope can be dragged left or right to change sweep speed from 4 to 24 revolutions per minute; a small readout prints “SWEEP 12 RPM / RANGE 800 M”. A log at right records the last four detections with timestamps: “03:14:22 — CONTACT 04 / WRECK / BEARING 217°”. Entrance: the sweep powers up from a single line and the scope rings appear one by one. Signature moment: the UNKNOWN contact changes bearing whenever the sweep has passed it three times, as if it is moving away. No emoji, no glass, no purple. Reduced motion renders a still scope.

DESCRIPTION
A phosphor sonar display with contacts that brighten as the sweep passes. One contact quietly changes its bearing each time the beam returns.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
Canvas rotating sweep with afterglow trail, range rings, contact detection and flare, draggable speed dial, timestamped contact log.

INTERACTION MODEL
Drag the rotary dial or use left/right arrows. A FREEZE control pauses the sweep.
