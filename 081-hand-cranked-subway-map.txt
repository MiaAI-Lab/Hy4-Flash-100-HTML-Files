TITLE
Line Nine — The Subway Map You Have to Crank

PROMPT
A crimson #B23B3B, cream #F2E9D8 and black #1C1C1C transit diagram for a fictional Line Nine. A hand-cranked wheel sits beside a single looping route drawn as a thin red line with eight stations. Turning the crank advances a black train dot along the line; each station it reaches unfolds its name in condensed capitals and stamps a time in the margin. On load the crank turns itself once and the train travels to the second station. Clicking a station jumps the train there. The wheel should feel mechanical: spokes rotate, a ratchet click sounds, and the margin times accumulate like a conductor's record. No emoji, no map tile imagery, no purple.

DESCRIPTION
A crank-driven subway route where turning a wheel moves the train and unfolds station names.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG route path with getPointAtLength, mechanical crank wheel, ratchet audio, staggered label reveal, stamped margin times.

INTERACTION MODEL
Drag the crank in circles or click it to advance; click any station to jump. Keyboard: arrow keys turn the crank. Reduced motion starts at the second station without animation.
