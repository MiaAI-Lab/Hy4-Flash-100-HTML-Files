TITLE
Typographic Orchard — Six Trees, No Fruit

PROMPT
An orchard of six thin trees drawn in cream #EDE6D2 on deep orchard green #557150, with fruit in one red #B53A2F. The trees are made entirely of letters: trunks are stacked ‘l’ characters, branches are diagonal runs of ‘/’ and ‘\\’, leaves are small lowercase words like ‘still’, ‘almost’, ‘after’. Hanging from the branches are twelve red circular fruits, each containing a single word in cream: WAIT, RIPEN, RETURN, QUIET, AGAIN, SOON, TODAY, GONE, HEARD, NEAR, YET, HOME. Clicking a tree shakes it: letters jitter, fruits detach and fall with simple gravity, bouncing once on the ground before settling into a woven basket at the bottom. Clicking a fallen fruit opens a one-line poem in a side panel. A SHAKE ALL button shakes every tree in a staggered wave. Text at upper left: “Nothing here is ripe. Everything here is waiting.” Entrance: trees type themselves into existence from the ground up; fruits fade in last. Signature moment: the falling fruits leave a brief trail of their own word, repeated small, like an echo. No emoji, no gradients, no purple. Reduced motion drops fruits directly into the basket.

DESCRIPTION
An orchard made of letters. Shaking the trees drops word-fruit into a basket, where each word reveals a tiny poem.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
DOM typography arranged as SVG-like tree structures, physics-based falling with bounce, echo trails, staggered shake animation, side-panel poems.

INTERACTION MODEL
Click a tree to shake it; click a fallen fruit to read its poem. SHAKE ALL shakes all six trees. Keyboard: trees and fruits are focusable buttons.
