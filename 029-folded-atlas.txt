TITLE
Folded Atlas — The Same Country, Two Sides

PROMPT
A washi-paper atlas sheet, #F4EFE4, centred on a deep ink #263E5C desktop. The front map is drawn only in vermilion #C8482F and ink: contour lines, a single river, six place names with invented, precise names like BELLS WITHOUT CLOCKS and LOW COUNTRY OF THE INNER POCKET. A horizontal crease divides the sheet. Dragging a small ink fold tab at the right edge folds the upper half down over the lower half in convincing 3D perspective, with a subtle shadow cast by the moving paper. As it folds, the front map disappears and the back of the folded half is revealed: the same geography at night, with pale chalk roads and tiny vermilion lights at each settlement. The title “FOLDED ATLAS / SHEET 29” sits upper left of the desktop in small monospaced spaced capitals. At the bottom: “A country fits in a pocket only when it is willing to become smaller.” When fully folded, the hidden map's lights come on one by one. A small RESET tab unfolds it. Entrance: the sheet unfolds from fully folded to flat on load over 1.4 seconds with an eased hinge. Signature moment: the same river appears continuous across front and back even though the paper is folded — the cartography refuses to obey the crease. No emoji, no buttons with default styling, no gradients on type.

DESCRIPTION
An interactive paper map. Folding the sheet reveals the same country after dark, proving that a place can be carried in two states at once.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
CSS preserve-3d folding panel with perspective, front/back faces with backface-visibility, generated SVG contour cartography, cast shadow driven by fold angle, staggered night-light reveal.

INTERACTION MODEL
Drag the fold tab vertically or use the range slider to fold and unfold. A reset control returns to flat. Keyboard: slider arrows control the fold.
