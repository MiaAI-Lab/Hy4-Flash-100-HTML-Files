TITLE
Bureau of Lost Buttons — A Coat With a Memory

PROMPT
A bureau drawer of lost buttons tipped out across a charcoal #2B2A27 desk. The drawer is a shallow mustard #D8A93C rectangle at the bottom; inside it sit forty small buttons drawn as circles with two or four holes, in bone #EEE7D6, brass #D8A93C and red #B64A34. At the centre hangs the outline of a coat in bone lines. Hovering a button reveals where it came from in a small monospaced caption: “OFFICE COAT, LEFT CUFF, 1987”, “SAILOR’S SHIRT, SECOND FROM TOP”. Clicking a button threads a needle: a thin bone line draws from the drawer to a specific position on the coat, the button flies along it and settles with a tiny rotation. The coat gradually fills with an impossible, non-symmetric constellation of buttons. A counter at upper right says REUNITED 00/40. Text at upper left: “Every button remembers the exact shape of the hole it left.” Entrance: buttons tumble out of the drawer into their places with staggered gravity. Signature moment: the stitching is drawn as individual dashed stitches, and each button lands at a slight angle determined by its origin. No emoji, no glass, no gradients. Reduced motion places buttons instantly.

DESCRIPTION
An inventory of lost buttons that can be sewn back onto a coat. Each button carries the memory of the garment it came from.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG button grid, JS path generation for dashed stitch lines, flight animation with landing rotation, live provenance caption, sparse coat silhouette.

INTERACTION MODEL
Hover to read a button's origin; click to sew it onto the coat. A RESET EMPTY COAT control removes all buttons. Keyboard: buttons are focusable and Enter sews.
