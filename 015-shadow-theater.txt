TITLE
Shadow Theater — A Play Performed by Objects Offstage

PROMPT
A black box theatre rendered as a single SVG stage. The top two-thirds are near-black #0D0B0A, the bottom is a pale lime-washed floor #F2E9D8 with a hard horizon line. Four small objects sit just above that line — a bottle, a chair, a bird, a key — drawn as flat black silhouettes, but each casts an enormous triangular shadow across the floor from a red light #E24A2B hanging above. Title “SHADOW THEATER” in tall serif capitals at upper left, with a small cast list beneath. A red draggable light handle sits on a horizontal rail near the ceiling. Dragging it left and right recomputes all four shadow polygons in real time: shadows shrink when the light is overhead and stretch, cross and overlap when it moves to an edge. Entrance: the light drops into place, the shadows snap long, then settle with a subtle elastic overshoot. Clicking an object or a cast-list row highlights it, dims the others, and shows a one-line stage direction in a lower margin (“The bottle refuses to be moved. Its shadow does not.”). Signature moment: when the light crosses an object's exact x-position, that object's shadow briefly inverts direction and reveals a thin red seam at its base. No gradients on type, no glass, no emoji. Reduced motion sets the light at centre without animation.

DESCRIPTION
An interactive shadow play in which unseen objects are known only by the shapes they throw. Moving the light makes the scene legible in a completely physical way.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG polygon projection calculated from a light point, pointer events on a rail and handle, elastic easing on initial light drop, opacity isolation per cast member, live stage-direction strip, high-contrast two-plane composition.

INTERACTION MODEL
Drag the red light along its rail; click or Tab to a cast member to isolate it. Keyboard arrows move the focused light handle.
