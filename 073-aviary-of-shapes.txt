TITLE
Aviary of Shapes — Sixty Birds, One Decision

PROMPT
A cream #F5EEDF field with sixty geometric birds drawn as turquoise #2E9C9A and coral #DC715D chevrons. They drift in a loose, noisy flock; hovering over them makes nearby birds tilt away from the pointer. A small printed command reading FORMATION / SCATTER sits in the corner. Press FORMATION and the birds lock into one precise arrowhead, arriving in staggered waves, wings briefly holding still before the flock resumes breathing. Signature moment: sixty independent shapes resolving into a single direction without colliding. No emoji, no feather illustrations, no gradient sky.

DESCRIPTION
A flock of geometric birds that can be scattered by the pointer or commanded into one precise arrowhead formation.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG chevron birds with inner wing flap, steering simulation, pointer avoidance, staggered formation targets, two-colour flock distribution.

INTERACTION MODEL
Move the pointer through the flock to disturb it; press FORMATION to assemble, SCATTER to release. Keyboard: the command is a button. Reduced motion snaps between states.
