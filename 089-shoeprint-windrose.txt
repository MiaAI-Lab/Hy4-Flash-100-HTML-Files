TITLE
Where the Wind Walked — A Shoeprint Windrose

PROMPT
A sand #D8C4A0, charcoal #30322F and chalk #F5EEE0 compass floor. A chalk windrose is drawn on the ground with eight spokes. Instead of an arrow, the prevailing wind is recorded as pairs of drawn shoeprints: on load a trail of six prints appears from the centre toward north, each landing with a small puff of dust. Clicking N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W or NW lays a fresh trail in that direction, alternating left and right feet, while the old trails fade to faint chalk. No emoji, no footprints photograph, no purple.

DESCRIPTION
A compass rose where wind direction is recorded as trails of drawn shoeprints.

VISUAL TECHNIQUES
SVG compass rose, generated sole shapes with alternating offsets, sequential landing animation, dust puff particles, chalk-fade trails.

INTERACTION MODEL
Click a direction label to lay a trail; press arrow keys or 1–8 to choose directions. Reduced motion shows the north trail immediately without dust.
