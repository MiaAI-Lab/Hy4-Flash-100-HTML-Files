TITLE
Long-Duration Wallpaper — Habitat Linen, Lot 06

PROMPT
A printed wallpaper sample for a spacecraft crew who will see the same wall for 1,100 days. Mint #CDE6DA, pink #E9BFC2 and cream #F7F2E8 form a woven repeat of orbit arcs, seed capsules, four-point stars and fine warp lines. The panel unrolls on load with a soft mechanical settle. Six small woven motif tiles sit along the bottom edge; clicking any one recolors the entire pattern through six considered palettes — ORBIT MINT, DUSK PEACH, SIGNAL RED, ALGAE, LUNAR BLUE, HULL GREY — with a visible wipe travelling across the cloth. Signature moment: the whole room changing mood from one tiny embroidered star. No emoji, no generic color-picker UI, no gradients.
